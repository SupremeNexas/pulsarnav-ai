Executive Summary

PulsarNav AI addresses the critical dependency of deep space exploration on Earth-based Deep Space Networks (DSN). By leveraging the rhythmic stability of millisecond pulsars, we establish a celestial GPS that operates independently of planetary infrastructure. This autonomous capability is paramount for missions beyond the heliopause.

The NANOGrav Dataset

Our platform integrates the 15-year dataset from the North American Nanohertz Observatory for Gravitational Waves. This dataset provides a rigorous timing model for 68 millisecond pulsars, accounting for interstellar medium dispersion and solar system ephemeris corrections.

Technical Specifications

ALGORITHM

Iterative Least-Squares Solver for 4D position estimation.

INTEGRATION

Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) for real-time trajectory refinement.

PRECISION

Sub-kilometer accuracy at 10 AU distance from Helios.
